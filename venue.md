The School will take place in Venice on the beautiful island of San Servolo in the Venice Lagoon, on the Campus of Venice International University ([VIU](http://univiu.org/campus-services/how-to-get-to-viu)), 10 minutes away from San Marco Square by public waterbus service.

Participants and invited speakers will be accommodated for the week on the Island, at the San Servolo [Residence](https://servizimetropolitani.ve.it/en/hospitality), offering an unique opportunity of a quiet and peaceful retreat in a location like Venice.
