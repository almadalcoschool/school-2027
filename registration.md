## Call for Applications

The **Alma Dal Co School on Pattern Formation in Biological Systems Across Scales** will take place on the island of San Servolo in Venice, Italy, from **September 12th to 17th, 2027**.

Participation in the school is subject to selection. The procedure is divided into two distinct phases:

---

### Phase 1: Call for Applications (Open Now)

Interested participants should apply by completing the online application form below:

👉 **[Online Application Form (Google Forms)](https://forms.gle/Ka2r93oEjcym7Se38)**

* **Application Deadline:** **January 31st, 2027**
* **Notification of Acceptance:** Chosen participants will be notified by **April 19th, 2027**

---

### Phase 2: Final Registration & Payment (After Selection)

The final registration and payment will be open **only to selected applicants** following notification on April 19th, 2027. 

* The payment and final registration will be managed directly through the official platform of **Venice International University (VIU)** under the supervision of **Silvia Casalini**.
* The registration payment link will be activated and sent directly to accepted participants after the selection.
* Starting now that the call is open, Silvia Casalini is available to answer any specific questions or requests from prospective applicants at [conferences@univiu.org](mailto:conferences@univiu.org).

---

### Fees & What is Included

The all-inclusive fee covers:

* **Accommodation for 5 nights** at the San Servolo Residence on the island (Check-in: **12 September 2027**, Check-out: **17 September 2027**)
* **All meals** (breakfasts, lunches, dinners, and coffee breaks) for the entire week
* **Full conference participation** and scientific sessions
* **Social and cultural events** (including concerts and visits)
* **Local transportation in Venice** (public waterbus / vaporetto service)

*(Note: Social events and public water transportation in Venice represent a significant cost and are fully included in the fee).*

**Fee Rates:**
* **Single room accommodation:** 1,500 €
* **Double room accommodation:** 1,150 €
